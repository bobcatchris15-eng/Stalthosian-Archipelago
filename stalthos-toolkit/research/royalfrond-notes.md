# Royal Frond (plant.royalfrond.fn1) — Evidence Notes

## Real Comparator
**Osmunda regalis** (Royal fern) — a living fern with large bipinnate fronds forming clumps in moist woodlands. Verified via GBIF (CC0). Distinct fertile pinnae (sorophores) at frond tips, giving a "flowering" appearance.
