# Bracketpolypore (plant.bracketpolypore.fg1) — Evidence Notes

## Real Comparator
**Fomitopsis pinicola** (Red-belted conk) — a common northern hemisphere polypore bracket fungus on conifer logs. Verified via GBIF (CC0). Typical perennial woody shelves with concentric zones, pore surface underneath.
