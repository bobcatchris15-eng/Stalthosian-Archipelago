# Softbough (plant.softbough.sh1) — Evidence Notes

## Real Comparator
**Salix alba** (White willow) and related riparian willows — fast-growing, multi-stemmed shrubs/trees with arching branches, lanceolate serrate leaves, common on stream banks. Verified via GBIF (CC0) at genus level (multiple species matches).

| Trait | Comparator Evidence | Confidence |

| Arching multi-stem architecture | Willows produce multiple basal stems that arch | High |

| Lanceolate serrate leaves | Salix leaves are lanceolate, finely serrate | High |
| Leaf length ~9 cm, aspect ratio ~5 | Typical for S. alba | Medium |

| Catkins (flowers) axillary | Salix flowers in catkins | High |
