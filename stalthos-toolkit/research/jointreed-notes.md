# Jointreed (plant.jointreed.eq1) — Evidence Notes

## Real Comparator
**Equisetum hyemale** (Rough horsetail / Scouring rush) — a living horsetail with tall, unbranched, jointed culms forming dense clonal thickets in wet soils. Verified via GBIF (CC0). High silica content, photosynthetic stems, whorled scale leaves at nodes.
