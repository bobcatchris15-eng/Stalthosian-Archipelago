# Basketfern (plant.basketfern.fn2) — Evidence Notes

## Real Comparator
**Marattia douglasii** (Hawaiian pala fern) — a living marattioid fern with giant once-pinnate fronds forming a vase-shaped crown from a short stout trunk. Verified via GBIF (CC0) and a CC BY 3.0 US photograph by Forest & Kim Starr (Wikimedia Commons).

| Trait | Comparator Evidence | Confidence |
