## Real Comparator
**Araucaria araucana** (Monkey puzzle tree) — an iconic living conifer with distinctive tiered whorled branching, sharp scale-like leaves, thick fissured bark, and a characteristic crown that often splits/forks in maturity. Verified via GBIF (CC0).
