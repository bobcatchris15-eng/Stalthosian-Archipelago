# Smalltrotter (creature.smalltrotter.st1) — Evidence Notes

## Real Comparator
**Saltasaurus** and **Opisthocoelicaudia** (titanosaurian sauropods) — Late Cretaceous dwarf/medium titanosaurs with known osteoderms (Saltasaurus) and relatively complete skeletons. Verified via Paleobiology Database (CC0). General titanosaur body plan: long neck, quadrupedal, columnar limbs, small skull.
