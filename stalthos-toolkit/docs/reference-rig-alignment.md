# Smalltrotter reference rig alignment

`build_creature` now stores a versioned `stalthos_rig_source_v1` JSON record on `body_proxy`. The record captures the resolved Smalltrotter profile dimensions and generated spine, neck, head, tail, and four limb joint paths, plus the actual foot-pad vertex indices. `rig_creature` validates its schema, finite shape values, joint coordinates, limb names, and in-range pad indices before using it. Bone placement, shape-dependent stride, and contact-pad selection therefore use the source asset's generated values. A source blend with no record follows the prior parameter-based skeleton and geometric pad-selection path.

The two reference detail meshes, `smalltrotter_eye_and_nostril_blockout` and `smalltrotter_bristle_ridge_blockout`, are included in armature binding. Their existing material slots are retained and vertices are assigned to the nearest head or body-chain bone, respectively. The rig report records binding, material-slot, vertex-group, unassigned-vertex, and neutral world-bound metrics.

The reference image at `references/creatures/smalltrotter/user-primary.png` guides the substantial rounded torso, broad limbs and feet, upright neck, and low tapered tail. It is a still image and provides no evidence of gait fidelity. Remaining review is needed for the exact silhouette and feature placement in rendered views; this change makes no visual-fidelity acceptance claim.

For `smalltrotter_reference_v1`, the profile-specific stride cap is `min(0.30 * trunk_length, 0.32 * (upper_leg + lower_leg))`; other profiles retain the existing 0.60 leg-length ratio. A seed-42 Blender 5.2 build-to-rig run measured 0.16128 m root stride, 0.0 m maximum serialized-joint/rest-bone endpoint error, 31 valid stance samples per hind pad within 4.086–10.163 mm of ground (20 mm tolerance), and touchdown-anchored horizontal excursion of 13.2513 mm left / 13.2512 mm right. Both hind pads passed the unchanged 20 mm checks. Eye and ridge geometry were armature-bound with one material slot and zero unassigned vertices each. These are focused pipeline measurements, not a full-suite or target-runtime result.
