# Bounded connected peer companionship draft

A calm body-local encounter can establish one peer link with one existing live
conspecific. PeerCompanionship owns a RelationshipTracker limited to two nodes
and one link each. No new actor, kinship, persisted field or contact session is
created. The connected host supplies the actual hero motion root, hero identity
and species. Camera coordinates never enter the encounter controller.

Proposed tuning in domain/peer_companionship.hpp: encounter9m, calm1.5 physical
seconds, retained observation/separation18m, wait3m. Formation also requires no
direct body input and measured body motion below.3m/s. Existing needs thresholds
must permit affiliation: hunger<=.42, thirst<=.48 and fatigue<=.62. These match
ordinary resource/rest selection. A busy peer retains its link but returns to
ordinary needs and awareness behavior. The cue reports nearby/needs/separated.
The relationship tracker records Present or Separated observations, rather than
inferring death or kinship from absence. Only one peer is retained this session.

An eligible peer uses the existing authored habitat graph and physical-time
movement at the existing.7m/s awareness speed. On a site arrival it chooses a
neighbor closer to the body, or waits/rests if no neighbor is closer or within3m.
An in-flight actor finishes its hop. Ordinary population needs/lifecycle advance
once, resource relief still requires existing shared-ledger eligibility/contact.
Other actors retain existing avoidance; the selected eligible peer can approach
instead. Normal hero direct controls and drink/browse/rest remain unchanged.
Original saved-mode behavior has no peer context. Connected preview still refuses
save/load; this link is explicitly session-only, not persisted relationship support.

-PeerCameraProbe places the body remotely with a detached camera near the actor.

existing animated body; its slow pace does not prove the wide proxy departure.
These are synthetic startup/input/render fixtures; normal play has none of those
overrides. JSON records actual actor coordinates, motion, peer identity and events;
captures peer-0..3.png show composed views/cues. No label-only friendship claim.
