# Reedcrest fourth life and one reciprocal peer

Proposed fictional working draft, October23,2026. `Play.ps1 -HeroSpecies Reedcrest`
selects an actual Reedcrest NeedsCore/Lifecycle and supported hero. Default and
other species remain selectable; the new startup option remains connected-only.
Original saved mode retains its own species restoration. Ordinary N begins another
selected-species life and clears the old hero's peer role/link without resetting
world actors, resources or sessions.

Replaceable basal hadrosauroid geometry has a barrel torso, short alert neck,
broad beaked head, long counterbalance tail, strong hindlimbs, smaller foraging
forelimbs, grounded feet and pale flank markings. It has no derived ornamental
crest. Generic support/+Z motion uses proposed1.6m/s forward/1.2reverse and simple
limb swing. This is provisional fixed-stage art, not authored anatomy/gait/IK.
Imported Smalltrotter remains NPC stand-in art, never the Reedcrest hero body.

Existing PeerCompanionship learns one actual conspecific through a calm local
encounter <=9m for1.5physical seconds. It retains that same pair identity during
body separation and reunion; camera position never enters learning or movement.
This is not a claimed kindergarten origin or2-4companion cohort.

When uncommanded and needs allow, the hero follows an eligible partner through
the existing HeroDestination/contextual owner to a point3m behind the actual NPC.
Only one movement role operates: player/direct/current intentional hero movement
or own urgent needs make the hero lead, otherwise the partner leads. Existing
player destinations and needs override the hero's own follow goal. Partner needs
can independently suspend association while the learned link remains.

Actual NPC response uses the existing single Population/registry advance. The
new BodyAwareness flags preserve learned identity/fear suppression while separately
allowing an NPC-led ordinary route or the existing hero-led graph approach. A
following reciprocal partner may reverse its current real edge by swapping
endpoint ownership with exact position unchanged; snapshots remain route-valid.
It stops physically3m from the hero even mid-edge, with idle/wait presentation.
No actor is ticked twice, relocated, respawned or assigned a new ID/session.

For urgent bonded NPC needs, the same near-body learned identity suppresses
ordinary fear in both population motion and the native contact eligibility loop.
This permits actual accepted resource contact/relief through its original session
and finite ledger; it is not a free needs grant. Existing history/bindings and
non-Reed default behavior remain. Dormant actors age once and do not follow/feed.
